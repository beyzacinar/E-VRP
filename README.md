E-VRP – Smart Electric Vehicle Route Planning & Charging Optimization

E-VRP (Electric Vehicle Routing Problem) is an intelligent route planning and charging optimization application designed to make electric vehicle journeys more efficient, convenient, and reliable.
The project aims to address common challenges faced by electric vehicle drivers, including limited driving range, charging station availability, battery management, and inefficient route planning.
By combining route optimization, battery consumption estimation, charging station information, and an intuitive mobile interface, E-VRP aims to help drivers make informed travel decisions while reducing range anxiety and unnecessary charging stops.

Key Objectives

Intelligent Route Planning: Identify efficient travel routes based on destination, battery level, vehicle range, and charging requirements.
Battery & Range Monitoring: Display battery status, estimated remaining driving range, and energy consumption.
Charging Station Discovery: Help users locate nearby charging stations and explore available charging options.
Charging Stop Optimization: Recommend suitable charging stops by considering route distance, charging power, and estimated charging duration.
Cost & Time Efficiency: Support route comparisons based on travel time, charging costs, and energy requirements.
Personalized Experience: Enable users to access favorite charging stations, previous routes, and relevant vehicle information.
Data-Driven Insights: Visualize charging activity, energy usage, and travel statistics to support better driving decisions.

Technology & Approach

E-VRP explores the integration of optimization algorithms, geographic information systems (GIS), battery consumption models, and charging infrastructure data.
The proposed system incorporates vehicle-specific battery parameters, charging station characteristics, and route information to support energy-aware journey planning and improve the overall electric vehicle driving experience.

Our Vision

Drive smarter. Charge efficiently. Travel confidently.
E-VRP aims to simplify electric vehicle mobility by making route planning more intelligent, charging decisions more convenient, and sustainable transportation more accessible.
